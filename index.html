Evidence over assumptions

  • Check sources and factual claims.
  • Ask students to explain key ideas.
  • Offer a fair opportunity to respond.

    REVIEW PRINCIPLES

    Responsible AI review guide

    Support learning, not automatic accusations.

    01 · Check the evidence

    Verify citations and claims directly. This prototype cannot check whether a source exists or supports a claim.

    02 · Ask, don't assume

    Ask the student to explain their argument, how they used sources, and why they chose a particular approach.

    03 · Apply policy consistently

    Check your institution's rules on permitted AI assistance, disclosure, collaboration, and assessment.

    04 · Give a fair opportunity

    Writing style and access to editing tools vary. Do not treat one indicator as proof of misconduct.
